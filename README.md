## About

Developer focused on AI automation and intelligent agent systems — from workflow design to production deployment.

I build and operate end-to-end automation stacks: multi-agent architectures orchestrated in n8n, LLM-powered workflows, WhatsApp automation via Chatwoot, lead qualification pipelines, and CRM integrations — connecting real business processes with production-grade infrastructure.

Currently developing AI agent systems for healthcare operations at a healthtech company, handling appointment scheduling, patient triage, and multi-channel integrations serving 30+ clinic clients.
